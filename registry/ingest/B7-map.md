## What this source is

The CIA-derived deception-detection methodology (Houston's Office of Security
polygraph lineage), written by practitioners. Where B5 detects stress and
evasion in live negotiation and B6 supplies clinical character reading, B7
supplies the **formal detection instrument**: a question-timed behavioural
model (five-second window, cluster rule, verbal + nonverbal indicator
catalogues), a lie-strategy taxonomy, and question-craft doctrine. Its claims
are practitioner-experiential, not laboratory findings; where it touches
compliance mechanisms B4 wins, where it touches strategy B3 wins.
