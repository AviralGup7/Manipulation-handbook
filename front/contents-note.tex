\chapter*{Contents}
\hbtopicmark{CONTENTS}
\begin{partblurb}
The list below is generated from the entry headers
(\texttt{registry/compiled/index.tex}), so it can never disagree with the
body of the book. Every entry line carries the entry's one-line thesis,
and every chapter line its strapline, so the contents read as the book's
argument in miniature: skim the contents and you have skimmed the claim
structure; follow any line into the body for the full entry.

At the end of Parts III through VII you will find a \emph{case}: a single
narrative situation --- a slow fraud, a keynote pitch, a difficult
neighbour, a fake job offer, a practitioner's own bill --- through which
several entries are run in sequence. Each move in a case names the entry
doing the work, so a case reads twice: once as a story, once as a map of
the lattice. Cases add no doctrine of their own; they are where the
entries meet.
\end{partblurb}
