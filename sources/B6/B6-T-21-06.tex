\dossier{B6}{T-21-06}{\bookshort{B6}}{ch. 9 (brandishing anger), pp. 122--123}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Against the conventional wisdom that anger is an involuntary emotion preceding aggression, B6's clinical finding: a deliberate display of anger can be a calculated and effective tool of intimidation, coercion and ultimately manipulation.
  \item The display works on the trained assumption that anger must be managed by accommodation: the target de-escalates by yielding, and the yield is the tactic's payout.
  \item Recognising performed anger does not require diagnosing sincerity --- judge the display by what it collects, and price the collection (T-24-06's rule applied to emotion).
\end{distilled}
