\begin{distilled}
  \item B6 describes the same machinery from the clinical side: the abuser's intermittent 'recovery' works like a slot machine --- a variable-ratio schedule in which the target's continued investing is periodically reinforced by a small jackpot of good behaviour, sustaining the hope that the big payoff is one more round away.
  \item The clinical case (Janice and Bill) is the dependence build observed in the wild: intermittent reward plus engineered guilt keeps the target investing in a ledger that only ever pays enough to prevent exit.
\end{distilled}
