\begin{distilled}
  \item The seduction tactic: melt resistance to giving loyalty and confidence by giving the target what he most needs to feel --- valued and important.
  \item B6 notes the gurus refined the tactic to an art: the manufactured specialness of being chosen, taught, elevated --- intimacy as an engineered product with a claim on the buyer.
  \item The defence reads the transaction: warmth that arrives pre-packaged around the target's exact unfilled needs is a sale, and the invoice is loyalty.
\end{distilled}
