\dossier{B9}{T-18-01}{\bookshort{B9}}{JASP 67(4):371--378; Method, Results}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Field measurement of the symbol mechanism at destructive scale: with nothing material behind the order --- university sponsorship, a laboratory, a title, a technician's coat, an impassive manner, and four spoken prods (no enforcement power whatever; refusal cost only the experiment) --- 26 of 40 subjects obeyed to the 450-volt maximum, against a predicted 1.2 percent.
  \item The symbols were precisely this entry's catalogue: the institutional setting, the role-title (``the experimenter''), the costume, and the flat vocabulary of routine (``The experiment requires that you continue''). Deference fired on the trappings and never once checked the substance, exactly as the entry claims.
  \item Where B4 supplies the trigger and B3 the costume, B9 measures the yield: the gap between predicted (1.2\%) and observed (65\%) obedience is the size of the blind spot about symbols.
\end{distilled}
