\dossier{B4}{T-23-01}{\bookshort{B4}}{ch. 4, pp. 126--156}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Audience reaction is a poor indicator of quality because it can be produced independently of the thing it appears to respond to.
  \item The recommended response is to evaluate the source of the reaction rather than the reaction.
  \item Staged agreement is characteristically uniform, synchronized and intolerant of dissent, where genuine agreement is uneven and reasoned.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The diagnostic shift: from asking whether the claim is true to asking what the room was built from.
  \item The observation that unanimity without reasons is itself the anomaly, since real agreement is unevenly held.
\end{unique}
