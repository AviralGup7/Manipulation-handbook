\dossier{B4}{T-23-02}{\bookshort{B4}}{ch. 5, pp. 157--177}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The components of liking are producible on demand, so warmth cannot be taken as evidence of regard.
  \item Manufactured resemblance is comprehensive where genuine resemblance is partial.
  \item Familiarity normally requires time; warmth arriving without the time has been supplied.
  \item The recommended response is to test the relationship against a small disagreement rather than to evaluate the claim being made.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The test by disagreement: rapport that survives a difference of opinion is real, and rapport that re-matches immediately is not.
  \item Directionality as a diagnostic --- manufactured warmth flows one way and asks nothing back.
\end{unique}
