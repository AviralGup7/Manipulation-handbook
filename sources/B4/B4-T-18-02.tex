\dossier{B4}{T-18-02}{\bookshort{B4}}{ch. 6, pp. 178--204}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Titles, uniforms and the visible trappings of position each trigger deference independently of the substance behind them.
  \item All three are detachable from what they signify and are cheap to acquire relative to genuine standing.
  \item Titles work best when borrowed from an adjacent field, where the vocabulary is recognised but the credential cannot be checked.
  \item Trappings are read as evidence of success, and success as evidence that deception was unnecessary.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The three symbols separated and compared by portability, cost and verifiability.
  \item The observation that each is engineered for a single contact and decays when the interaction is extended over time.
\end{unique}
