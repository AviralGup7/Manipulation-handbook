\dossier{B4}{T-24-02}{\bookshort{B4}}{ch. 3, pp. 87--125}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The recommended defence is to separate the reasons that preceded a commitment from those that followed it, since the latter are largely generated by the commitment.
  \item Consistency is defended as character rather than re-examined as judgement, and the pressure to be consistent is social as much as internal.
  \item Reversal becomes affordable once it is reclassified as a response to new information rather than as inconsistency.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The before-and-after test on a person's own reasons as a diagnostic for manufactured commitment.
  \item The observation that a withdrawn inducement leaving the position intact is itself the evidence that the position was installed.
\end{unique}
