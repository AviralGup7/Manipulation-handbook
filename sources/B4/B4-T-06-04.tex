\dossier{B4}{T-06-04}{\bookshort{B4}}{ch. 3, pp. 87--125}

\begin{distilled}
  \item A stated position begins to generate its own support, and is defended after the grounds for taking it have been removed.
  \item Conduct following a declaration is therefore not independent evidence of the person's view; some of it is the declaration maintaining itself.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The direction of the effect: consistency pressure means declarations cause later behaviour, not only report it. This complicates the reading rule in the entry rather than confirming it, and is recorded as such.
\end{unique}
