\dossier{B4}{T-14-04}{\bookshort{B4}}{ch. 3, pp. 87--125}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Commitments that are written, active, public and effortful produce the most durable changes in behaviour.
  \item Writing binds because it creates a record and because authoring a statement is a stronger act than receiving one.
  \item Effort is the strongest amplifier: what has cost much is valued highly, and the valuation is generated to justify the cost.
  \item Public commitments become statements of identity, and are defended as such rather than re-evaluated as preferences.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The four properties named as a single design space, and the observation that they compound.
  \item Effort as a generator of value rather than merely a cost of it.
\end{unique}
