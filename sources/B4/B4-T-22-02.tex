\dossier{B4}{T-22-02}{\bookshort{B4}}{ch. 5, pp. 157--177}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Regard transfers through association: contact with something valued raises the value of what it is next to.
  \item The transfer operates where information is unavailable and is experienced as though it were a judgement on the merits.
  \item The same mechanism produces a consistent linguistic asymmetry --- first-person claims on success, distant descriptions of failure.
  \item Messenger selection, setting and timing are all described as uses of association.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The linguistic asymmetry as a diagnostic: the way a person talks about outcomes they did not produce reveals the mechanism operating in them.
  \item Association treated as a controllable variable --- messenger, setting, timing --- rather than as a matter of luck.
\end{unique}
