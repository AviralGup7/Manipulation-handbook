\begin{distilled}
  \item The three activities differ in what the target is left holding, and the mechanism view sharpens the line: an edited picture can be delivered by one manufactured feature rather than by an argument, which is what makes manipulation cheap and difficult to detect from inside.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}

\end{unique}
