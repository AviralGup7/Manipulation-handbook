\dossier{B4}{T-23-03}{\bookshort{B4}}{ch. 6, pp. 178--204}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The recommended defence against false authority is to separate the question of genuine expertise from the question of whether the expert is being straight in this instance.
  \item Verification must reach the institution by a route the claimant does not control.
  \item Urgency is used specifically to make verification impractical.
  \item Reasoning can be requested from anyone at no cost, and separates real expertise from its symbols.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The circularity diagnosis: a check performed through channels supplied by the claimant is not a check. This is the structural insight the entry is built on and is not present in B1 or B3.
  \item Unverifiable authority treated as absent rather than as presumed.
\end{unique}
