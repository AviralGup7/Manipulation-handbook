\dossier{B4}{T-04-03}{\bookshort{B4}}{ch. 5, pp. 157--177}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Praise raises compliance even where the recipient judges it inaccurate or strategic.
  \item Being approved of creates an inclination to approve in return, independently of whether the approval was deserved.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The durability of the handle under recognition: knowing the flattery is flattery does not disable it. This strengthens the entry's claim that the handle is reliable rather than merely common, and is recorded because neither B1 nor B3 makes it.
\end{unique}
