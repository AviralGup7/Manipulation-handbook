\dossier{B8}{T-23-04}{\bookshort{B8}}{PSPR 10(3):214--234; Results (accuracy, expertise), Discussion (double standard)}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Calibration for the alarm: pooled over 24,483 judges, unaided reads land at 53.98\% accuracy --- the gut's confidence is not a track record. Experts did no better than novices ($d = -.025$), so seasoning the feeling adds nothing measurable.
  \item The stereotype the gut reasons with --- the liar as tormented, fidgeting, gaze-averse --- is a moral caricature; calm, rationalized deceit fails to match it and passes, while the anxious honest are flagged. The alarm fires most surely at exactly the wrong targets.
  \item The audit step is the documented lever: judges with baseline exposure to the sender reached 55.91\% against 52.26\% without it ($d = .239$). The feeling selects; only records and baselines confirm.
\end{distilled}
