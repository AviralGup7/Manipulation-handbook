\dossier{B1}{T-01-05}{\bookshort{B1}}{ch. 1}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Effectiveness is stated as the sole evaluation criterion for a tactic: it must work in practice.
  \item The author's education is described as coming from practitioners --- hustlers and con artists --- rather than from academic sources, on the ground that theory had not produced usable method.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The explicit rejection of academic framing in favour of field effectiveness, as the book's stated epistemology.
\end{unique}

\begin{terms}
  \item \emph{the nut} --- a business's fixed monthly overhead; used later as the constraint on how independent a person can afford to appear.
\end{terms}
