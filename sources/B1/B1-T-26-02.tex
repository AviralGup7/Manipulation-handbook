\dossier{B1}{T-26-02}{\bookshort{B1}}{ch. XVI, pp. 177--182}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The cost chapter's pricing rule: grab for too much on one deal and you will be cut out of the next one; people catch on and end the relationship.
  \item Conduct changes very little from one relationship to the next --- mistreat some people and you will mistreat anyone; the trait, not the target, is the unit.
  \item The eagle-and-the-ice image: the once-only snatch sticks, and the bird goes over the falls with the block; each selfish act makes the next feel natural.
  \item The fix is an exit designed in advance: enough defined before the wanting starts, the counterparty left whole --- repeat business as the dividend of restraint.
\end{distilled}
