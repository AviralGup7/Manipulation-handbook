\dossier{B1}{T-04-02}{\bookshortB1}{Tactic 14, pp. 167--176}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Ingratitude is treated as a predictable outcome rather than as a moral failure, and the chapter is about preventing it in advance rather than punishing it afterwards.
  \item The premise is that a benefit conferred creates an expectation which the recipient experiences as pressure, and that pressure converts into resentment if it is left unstated.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item Framing ingratitude as a structural problem with a preventive technique, rather than as a character judgement about the recipient.
\end{unique}
