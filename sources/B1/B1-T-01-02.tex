\dossier{B1}{T-01-02}{\bookshort{B1}}{ch. 1 and ch. 12--13}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Sparkman separates talking someone into something from arranging the situation so they talk themselves into it; his tactics are overwhelmingly the second kind.
  \item The pressure tactics (12 and 13) are treated as a distinct, last-resort register --- a little pressure has its place but seldom --- which is coercion, not manipulation, and is priced accordingly.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The explicit ranking of registers: manipulate by arrangement first, apply pressure rarely, and treat the openly dirty method as a last resort with a known cost.
\end{unique}
