\dossier{B1}{T-04-04}{\bookshortB1}{Tactic 1; Tactic 6, pp. 71--76}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The book's premise is that the reader is on the receiving end far more often than they realise, and that the remedy is to learn the tactics in order to recognise them.
  \item Determining a person's real motive is treated as a prerequisite to influencing them; the same reasoning applies reflexively to one's own motives.
  \item Knowing what obstacle actually stands in your way is presented as the difference between effective action and swinging in the dark.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The explicit instruction to learn the offensive material for defensive purposes, stated as the book's reason for existing.
\end{unique}
