\dossier{B1}{T-12-01}{\bookshort{B1}}{Tactic 3 (ch. IV), pp. 31--42}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Top young executives, asked what counted most in their rise, answered getting in good with the boss --- over work and performance.
  \item Favouritism is presented as how hierarchies actually transmit reward, not a corruption of them.
  \item Refusing to attach does not exempt a person; it leaves the benefit to whoever does attach.
  \item The countermove for gatekeepers: the objection method --- listen, listen, listen --- turns the favour-seeker's own account into the decision material.
\end{distilled}
