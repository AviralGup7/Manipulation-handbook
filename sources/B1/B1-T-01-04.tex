\dossier{B1}{T-01-04}{\bookshort{B1}}{ch. 1, closing; ch. 16}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The ethics of the tactic set are located in intent rather than in the technique: the same moves serve self-defence and exploitation.
  \item The stated purpose of teaching them is to equip the reader to recognise and refuse malicious use.
  \item The final chapter prices the practice: what a person ends up with after using people for their own ends.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The closing cost chapter, which treats the outcome for the practitioner as part of the technique's description rather than as a moral aside.
\end{unique}
