\dossier{B1}{T-26-01}{\bookshort{B1}}{Tactic 14 (ch. XV), pp. 167--176}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The author's first business year: nursing a drunk who repaid him by pursuing his woman friend; staking a start-up that repaid him with one hour's notice to vacate and a swaggered refusal to repay the loan.
  \item Society's teaching --- constant goodness and sacrifice make a friend who returns good things --- is presented as the first thing the jungle kills.
  \item Gratitude is not a function of benefits received; predictable kindness becomes baseline, and baseline feels earned.
  \item The cure is the intermittent schedule of Tactic 2 applied to existing relationships: keep what they receive tied to what they need, sized and timed unpredictably.
\end{distilled}
