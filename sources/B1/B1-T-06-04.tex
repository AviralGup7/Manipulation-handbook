\dossier{B1}{T-06-04}{\bookshortB1}{Tactic 1, pp. 13--16}

\begin{distilled}
  \item A person who genuinely holds a position does not feel a nervous need to remind you of it; their behaviour in the relationship speaks for itself.
  \item Words are described as compensation for a lack of substance.
  \item An example is given of a strongly declared social position adopted by someone whose conduct did not support it, read as an attempt to use the listener.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The over-declaration diagnostic in personal rather than commercial relationships: repeated affirmation of a stance as compensation for absent substance.
\end{unique}
