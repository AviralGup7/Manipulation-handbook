\dossier{B1}{T-27-01}{\bookshort{B1}}{ch. XVI, pp. 177--180}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The author's gallery of ruthless operators: not one had a real friend; charm won women who wised up within weeks; every marriage ended in divorce.
  \item His prediction for each: the only person in attendance at his own funeral.
  \item Hardy drank, the author notes, not for pleasure --- the pattern of use ran through the user as well.
  \item The chapter is explicitly argued from self-interest, not morality: the third course is a bad trade even on the operator's own ledger.
\end{distilled}
