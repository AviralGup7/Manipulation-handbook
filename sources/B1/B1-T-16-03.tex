\dossier{B1}{T-16-03}{\bookshort{B1}}{Tactic 10 (ch. XI), pp. 117--132}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The rule of thumb, framed as maturity: the strength to let a useless argument go, above all when sure of being right.
  \item Arguments are scoped by vital interests --- mental, physical, financial well-being, one's people; outside that scope they are display.
  \item Letting sleeping dogs lie preserves composure, time and standing, the assets arguing burns.
  \item The claim of the chapter title --- winning the large majority of arguments --- is made precisely by declining the ones that change nothing.
\end{distilled}
