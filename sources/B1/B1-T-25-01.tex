\dossier{B1}{T-25-01}{\bookshort{B1}}{Tactic 8 (ch. IX), pp. 89--108 (additive)}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Once a contractor has your money in advance, he faces no real compulsion to lift another finger; payment before delivery is the trap's mechanism.
  \item When somebody asks for any payment in advance other than a small deposit, alarms should go off: that is a deadly financial trap being laid.
  \item The folly of okaying goods or services before asking the price: the man who said do whatever it takes received a $600 bill expecting $200 --- strike a price agreement prior to giving your okay.
  \item Being too shy to ask prices is a long-haul tax; the money saved always compensates the minor social discomfort.
\end{distilled}
\begin{unique}
  \item B1's ask-the-price-first doctrine and the deposit rule are unique to B1 in this corpus.
\end{unique}
