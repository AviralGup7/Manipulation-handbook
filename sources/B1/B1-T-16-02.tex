\dossier{B1}{T-16-02}{\bookshort{B1}}{Tactic 9 (ch. X), pp. 109--116}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The chapter premise: know which minds can be changed, and when silence is the better move.
  \item Minds are terrain --- a forest a truck must be driven through; know which trees bend and which will total the truck.
  \item Effort spent on an immovable mind strengthens it and spends the persuader's credibility funding the target's certainty.
  \item The movable mind is one caught between yes and no --- ambivalent, recently surprised by consequences, socially unanchored.
\end{distilled}
