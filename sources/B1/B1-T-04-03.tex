\dossier{B1}{T-04-03}{\bookshort{B1}}{Tactic 5; Tactic 10, pp. 117--124}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Ego injury is identified as the trigger that produces persistent enemies, which is the practical reason for avoiding the clever putdown.
  \item Most arguments are said to be winnable by declining to contest the point that carries the other person's standing, and letting sleeping dogs lie where your vital interests are unaffected.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The rule of thumb that an argument should only be contested where it touches your vital interests, with everything else conceded as cheaper than the resentment.
\end{unique}
