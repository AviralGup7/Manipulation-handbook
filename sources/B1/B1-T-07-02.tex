\dossier{B1}{T-07-02}{\bookshortB1}{Tactic 6, pp. 71--76}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Discovering a person's real motives and feelings is treated as a technique with specific procedures rather than as intuition.
  \item The direct question has a place, but only when paired with observation of the immediate involuntary reaction, because the words that follow are managed and the first response is not.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item Pairing the direct question with observation of the involuntary response, on the reasoning that managed words and unmanaged reactions arrive in a fixed order.
\end{unique}
