\dossier{B1}{T-11-01}{\bookshort{B1}}{Tactic 2 (ch. III), pp. 19--30}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Constant giving trains the recipient to treat it as baseline; only irregular reward produces compulsive pursuit.
  \item The suitor case: reward delivered unpredictably keeps the target working for tokens she can never count on.
  \item The trainer's analogy: an animal rewarded every time works only while the reward is in sight; rewarded at random, it works always.
  \item Dependence, not affection, is the load-bearing element --- affection fluctuates, structure does not.
\end{distilled}
