\dossier{B3}{T-17-04}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 25, pp. 191--200}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Do not accept the roles that society foists on you; re-create yourself by forging a new identity that commands attention and never bores the audience.
  \item Be the master of your own image rather than letting others define it for you.
  \item Incorporate dramatic devices into your public gestures and actions; your character will seem larger than life.
  \item Reversal: there is no reversal --- but bad theatre is bad theatre, and even appearing natural requires art; overacting is the only sin named.
\end{distilled}
