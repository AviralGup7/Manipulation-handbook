\dossier{B3}{T-07-02}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 14, pp. 107--114}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Indirect questions are the recommended instrument in social encounters, used to make people reveal weaknesses and intentions.
  \item Probing is described as a learnable behaviour in polite company rather than as a special skill of interrogators.
\end{distilled}
