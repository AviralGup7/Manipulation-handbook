\dossier{B3}{T-15-04}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 32, pp. 263--270}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The truth is often avoided because it is ugly and unpleasant; never appeal to truth and reality unless prepared for the anger that disenchantment brings.
  \item Life is so harsh that those who can manufacture romance or conjure up fantasy are like oases in the desert: everyone flocks to them.
  \item There is great power in tapping into the fantasies of the masses; the supply of better-feeling material competes on feeling, not accuracy.
  \item The closing concession: fantasy need not be fantastical --- reality itself is sometimes so theatrical and stylised (the homespun Lincoln image) that staging simple truths performs the same work.
\end{distilled}
