\dossier{B3}{T-15-01}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 13, pp. 59--64 (additive)}

\begin{distilled}
  \item When asking for help, appeal to self-interest, never to mercy or gratitude: uncover what will benefit the person and emphasize it.
  \item Applied to crowds: people join a moving crowd when joining serves them --- safety, belonging, plausible deniability --- so a manufactured crowd works by making membership privately beneficial.
  \item The crowd's evidence is therefore always priced: ask what each joiner is buying before you read the queue as proof.
\end{distilled}
