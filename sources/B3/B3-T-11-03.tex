\dossier{B3}{T-11-03}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 11, pp. 85--90 (keys and keys-side cases)}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Two architectures of dependence: intensive (Michelangelo's one irreplaceable skill) and extensive (Kissinger entrenched in so many departments that removing him meant chaos).
  \item The chain variant: Harry Cohn kept the blacklisted screenwriter because the star depended on him --- indispensable in someone else's chain inherits protection.
  \item The corollary: never teach them enough so that they can do without you; the teaching rate is the dependence dial.
  \item The image is vines with many thorns --- entwined so widely that removal costs toil and blood, so they are left to climb.
  \item Authority: he who has slaked his thirst immediately turns his back on the well.
\end{distilled}
