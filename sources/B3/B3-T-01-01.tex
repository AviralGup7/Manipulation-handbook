\dossier{B3}{T-01-01}{\bookshortB3}{preface; ch. Amoral power}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Power is presented as a game that runs whether or not you choose to play, and refusing to play is itself a move with consequences.
  \item The book addresses the practitioner and the target in the same voice, on the argument that the techniques are identical from both sides of the table.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The explicit amoral framing: the mechanics of power are treated as descriptive, and moral judgement is moved to the reader's choice of use.
\end{unique}
