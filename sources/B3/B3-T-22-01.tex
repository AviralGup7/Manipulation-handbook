\dossier{B3}{T-22-01}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 43, pp. 367--378 (additive)}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Work on the hearts and minds of others: appeal to their emotions and their weakest reasoning powers; the hearts win first, the minds are dragged after.
  \item Association with the target's ideas and dreams --- speak in visceral metaphors, voice their deepest anxieties, let them vent.
  \item Self-interest is the solid foundation beneath the emotion: a great cause captures minds, but interest flags unless there is something to be gained; the causes that work best use a noble veneer to cover a blatant appeal to self-interest.
  \item Authority: know the heart of the persuaded and fit your wording into it --- persuasion without reading the target is noise.
\end{distilled}
