\dossier{B3}{T-12-01}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 23, pp. 175--177}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The young courtier rises by attaching to the king's mother while she, not the king, decides things.
  \item Strike oil once --- one strong attachment can secure a lifetime; the arrow image: one arrow, one target, thought and arrow one.
  \item Concentration of attachment is presented as the alternative to spreading across many camps, which leaves a person owned by none.
\end{distilled}
