\dossier{B3}{T-18-03}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 21, pp. 155--164}

\begin{distilled}
  \item No one likes feeling stupider than the next person; the trick is to make your victims feel smart --- and not just smart, but smarter than you are.
  \item Once convinced of their superiority, they will never suspect you have ulterior motives.
  \item The con-artist observances run the pattern to the catch: the mark, convinced of fleecing a fool, carries the operator's plan as personal cleverness.
  \item Reversal warns the performance is risky: condescension detected re-arms the one alarm the technique exists to disarm.
\end{distilled}
