\dossier{B3}{T-18-01}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 12, pp. 49--54 (additive)}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Use selective honesty and generosity to disarm your victim: one sincere and honest move covers over dozens of dishonest ones.
  \item Open-hearted gestures bring down the guard of even the most suspicious people; once the selective honesty opens a hole in their armour, they can be manipulated at will.
  \item For the trappings entry: the symbol raises the target's guard and the single disarming gesture lowers it --- authority is most effective on exactly the people it intimidates, which is when the honest-seeming move is timed.
\end{distilled}
