\dossier{B3}{T-25-02}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 40, pp. 337--346}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Despise the free lunch: what arrives without a price arrives with a hook, and judging by the wrong criterion --- cheapness --- invites ruin.
  \item The fable: the tiger raised on free meat does not stay tame; the trap sprung is the one that was fed first.
  \item Unpaid benefits carry three payloads at once: obligation, disarmament, and the classification of the receiver as purchasable.
  \item The rule extends to one's own conduct: pay what you are worth --- discounts are self-selling on instalments, and everything bought cheap arrives with advice attached.
\end{distilled}
