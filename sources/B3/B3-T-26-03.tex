\dossier{B3}{T-26-03}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 10, pp. 73--80}

\begin{distilled}
  \item You can die from someone else's misery --- emotional states are as infectious as diseases.
  \item You may feel you are helping the drowning man, but you are only precipitating your own disaster; the unfortunate draw misfortune on themselves and will draw it on you.
  \item Associate with the happy and fortunate instead; the Lola Montez trail shows careers and tempers ruined by proximity to turbulence.
  \item The diagnostic trap: infectors often present as victims, so their miseries read as undeserved until you are already entangled.
\end{distilled}
