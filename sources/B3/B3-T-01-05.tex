\dossier{B3}{T-01-05}{\bookshortB3}{preface; structure of each law}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Each law is supported by historical cases of transgression and observance, i.e. argued from outcomes rather than from principle.
  \item The reversal section of each law states when the rule should be broken, which is an acknowledgement that outcome-based rules are context-bound.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The transgression/observance/reversal case structure as an argumentative form: a rule is justified by what happened to people who broke it and to people who kept it, then bounded by the conditions under which it inverts.
\end{unique}
