\dossier{B3}{T-15-03}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 27, pp. 213--222}

\begin{distilled}
  \item People have an overwhelming desire to believe in something; become the focal point of that desire by offering a cause, a new faith to follow.
  \item Keep your words vague but full of promise; emphasize enthusiasm over rationality and clear thinking.
  \item Give disciples rituals to perform and ask for sacrifices on your behalf; in the absence of organized religion the new belief system brings untold power.
  \item The charlatan's market is the credulous disposition; the group enlarges to mass proportions once belief is behaviour.
\end{distilled}
