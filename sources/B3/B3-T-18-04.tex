\dossier{B3}{T-18-04}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 38, pp. 313--322}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Think what you like; behave like those around you --- visible difference draws discipline long before it draws evaluation.
  \item The Campanella case: the philosopher under a death sentence stopped writing declarations and survived by service to the power that held him, the doctrine intact and unannounced.
  \item A slight affectation of past eras may charm; current fashion is the conformity that buys the hearing.
  \item The heresy is preached only after the win is secured, when the audience can no longer unmake the preacher.
\end{distilled}
