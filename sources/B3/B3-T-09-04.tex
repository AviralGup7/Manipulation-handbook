\dossier{B3}{T-09-04}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 46, pp. 399--409}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Appearing better than others is always dangerous; most dangerous is appearing to have no faults or weaknesses.
  \item Envy creates silent enemies; the smart occasionally display defects and admit harmless vices to deflect envy and seem human and approachable.
  \item Only gods and the dead can seem perfect with impunity.
  \item The law's transgression is the envy-destroyed partnership; its observance shows defects deliberately left visible as armour.
\end{distilled}
