\dossier{B3}{T-02-04}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 30, pp. 245--251}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Actions must appear natural and easy; the toil, the practice and the clever tricks behind them are concealed.
  \item Revealing how hard you work is described as raising questions rather than earning credit.
  \item The instruction ends with a warning about teaching: reveal your tricks and they will be used against you.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The explicit link between concealing method and security --- a visible method is a method that can be turned back on its owner.
\end{unique}
