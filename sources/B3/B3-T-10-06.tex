\begin{distilled}
  \item Striking imagery and grand symbolic gestures create the aura of power --- everyone responds to them.
  \item Stage spectacles full of arresting visuals and radiant symbols that heighten presence; dazzled by appearances, no one audits what is really being done.
  \item Cleopatra's barge: a political negotiation arriving as a sensory event, retold for two thousand years.
  \item Reversal: none --- no power is made available by ignoring images and symbols.
\end{distilled}
