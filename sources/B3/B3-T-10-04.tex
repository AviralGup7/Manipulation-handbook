\dossier{B3}{T-10-04}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 6, pp. 55--60}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Everything is judged by appearance; what is unseen counts for nothing.
  \item Never let yourself get lost in the crowd or buried in oblivion: stand out, be conspicuous at all cost.
  \item Make yourself a magnet of attention by appearing larger, more colourful, more mysterious than the next person.
  \item The court-attention law pairs with the source's association trick: attention attaches to a name, and the name carries the substance unknown.
\end{distilled}
