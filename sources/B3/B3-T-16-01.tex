\dossier{B3}{T-16-01}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 9, pp. 43--48 (additive)}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Win through your actions, never through argument: a momentary triumph won by arguing is a Pyrrhic victory.
  \item The resentment and ill will stirred by argument outlasts any change of opinion it produced; demonstration lets the deed make the concession's case without a rebuttal to defend against.
  \item The law's mechanism for the retreat: deeds persuade without creating the point to attack, so the counterpart concedes to evidence instead of to you.
\end{distilled}
