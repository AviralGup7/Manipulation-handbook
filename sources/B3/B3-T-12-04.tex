\dossier{B3}{T-12-04}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 7, pp. 55--60}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Use the wisdom, knowledge and legwork of others to further your cause; it saves time and energy and gives a godlike aura of efficiency and speed.
  \item In the end your helpers will be forgotten and you will be remembered: never do yourself what others can do for you.
  \item Tesla--Edison: the genius ran through Edison's operation and Edison's name absorbed the credit --- the patronage that grounds a man also grounds him to the earth.
  \item Edison's own habit ran the law backward and forward at once: take credit for the work of those below, graciously cede credit for his own labours to those above.
\end{distilled}
