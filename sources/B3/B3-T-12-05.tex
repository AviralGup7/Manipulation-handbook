\dossier{B3}{T-12-05}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 24, pp. 181--190}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Master your emotions: cry and laugh on command, disguise frustration, fake contentment --- be the master of your own face.
  \item Fit the spirit of the times: a slight affectation of an era twenty years back may charm; last decade's fashion is ludicrous; thinking must keep up with the court you are in.
  \item Be a source of pleasure: people flee the unpleasant and draw toward delight; make yourself the flame and you become indispensable as food and drink.
  \item The court as the model environment: one principal's moods set everyone's weather, and every reaction leak is ammunition for rivals.
\end{distilled}
