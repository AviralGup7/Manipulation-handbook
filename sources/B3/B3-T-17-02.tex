\dossier{B3}{T-17-02}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 28, pp. 233--242}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Enter with spirit or do not enter at all: timidity at the threshold invites contempt and prices everything after.
  \item Audiences resolve the gap between nerve and evidence upward --- boldness wins respect before evidence arrives.
  \item The timid do more harm than good; but boldness is lion-hearted, not foolhardy --- a reasonable man may act like a madman, never like an idiot.
  \item Boldness is an entry tool: once established, audacity reads as arrogance and quiet competence is the premium currency.
\end{distilled}
