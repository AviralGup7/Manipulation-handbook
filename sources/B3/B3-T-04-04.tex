\dossier{B3}{T-04-04}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 33; Law 39; Law 48}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The weak-point analysis is directed at the counterpart, but the same enumeration applies to the reader.
  \item Emotional reaction is identified as the thing that hands control to an opponent, which makes knowing your own triggers the precondition of the calm prescribed elsewhere.
  \item Formlessness --- refusing to have a fixed shape an opponent can attack --- is the end state of the same discipline.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item Formlessness as the terminal goal of self-knowledge: having no fixed shape is presented as the only durable defence, since a known point can always be loaded.
\end{unique}
