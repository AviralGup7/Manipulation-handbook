\dossier{B3}{T-01-02}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 43; Law 9}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Working on hearts and minds is preferred to force because coercion produces a reaction that eventually turns against the user.
  \item Winning through argument is rejected as a Pyrrhic result: the resentment outlasts the change of opinion. Demonstration is preferred to explication.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The durability argument: consent obtained by working on a person's own psychology persists without maintenance, while compliance obtained by force must be enforced continuously.
\end{unique}
