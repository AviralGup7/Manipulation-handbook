\dossier{B3}{T-11-01}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 11, pp. 85--90}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The court astrologer tells a kill-happy king he will die three days before him; the king, unable to test the prediction without risking the man, enriches and protects him for life.
  \item The model: make others dependent on you --- removal must spell disaster.
  \item Foremost method: possess a talent that cannot be replaced; Michelangelo could find another patron but the pope could not find another Michelangelo.
  \item Real dependence is stronger than the faked kind, but the appearance of specialised knowledge already buys leeway.
\end{distilled}
