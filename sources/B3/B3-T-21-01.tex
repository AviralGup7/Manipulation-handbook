\dossier{B3}{T-21-01}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 15, pp. 109--118}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Crush the enemy totally or leave him be: the half-crushed rebuilds, and revenge is the one human project pursued without opportunity cost.
  \item The canon case: the condottieri left alive after a half-won fight --- Vitellozzo and Orsini --- were strangled the same night precisely because half-measures had failed before.
  \item A defeated opponent loses property and face together, and remembers both; recovery becomes their life's meaning.
  \item Mercy priced as generosity at victory usually purchases a future enemy at a discount; total reconciliation exists but is a full design, not an absence of finishing.
\end{distilled}
