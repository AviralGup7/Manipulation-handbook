\dossier{B3}{T-02-03}{\bookshortB3}{Law 35, pp. 291--299}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Hurrying is described as betraying a lack of control over yourself and over time; the instruction is to appear patient, as though everything will arrive eventually.
  \item The practitioner is told to become a detective of the right moment and to read the spirit of the times.
  \item The skill is stated as a pair: stand back when the time is not ripe, strike fiercely when it is.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item Timing treated as a learnable perceptual skill --- detecting the ripe moment --- rather than as a matter of waiting passively.
\end{unique}
