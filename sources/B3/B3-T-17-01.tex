\dossier{B3}{T-17-01}{\bookshort{B3}}{Laws 35 and 29; pp. 293--302, 243--252}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Master timing: detect the spirit of the times and move with them; those who rush into action at the wrong time pay for it.
  \item Visible hurry is weakness: the unhurried seem in control, and desire concealed is desire not taxed.
  \item Patience at the unripe stage, then fierce action at the ripened one; a ripe moment struck softly is wasted.
  \item Plan to the end: execute along a line that closes with the objective, and end the move when the objective is reached.
\end{distilled}
