\dossier{B3}{T-14-06}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 31, pp. 253--262}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The best deceptions seem to give the other person a choice: your victims feel they are in control but are your puppets.
  \item Give people options that come out in your favour whichever they choose; force them to make choices, but between roads you built.
  \item Offer alternatives, never ultimatums: ultimatums invite the counter-move of defiance; a menu satisfies the autonomy reflex.
  \item The excluded option is the mechanism's core: the strongest alternative is never tabled, so it is never argued against.
\end{distilled}
