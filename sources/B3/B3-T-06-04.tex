\dossier{B3}{T-06-04}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 9, pp. 69--75; Law 4}

\begin{distilled}
  \item A triumph gained through argument is Pyrrhic: the resentment outlasts the change of opinion, so agreement produced by demonstration is worth more than agreement produced by words.
  \item Demonstration is preferred to explication.
  \item Saying less than necessary is linked to the same principle: the more words, the more common you appear and the less in control, and the more likely you are to say something foolish.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The resentment-cost calculation attached to verbal victory, which is why behavioural demonstration is preferred on effectiveness grounds rather than on stylistic ones.
\end{unique}
