\dossier{B3}{T-21-03}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 36, pp. 303--312}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Disdain things you cannot have: refusing to want the unattainable converts it from prize to irrelevance.
  \item Schopenhauer's advice: let dependents feel, now and then, that your company could be dispensed with --- a grain of disdain consolidates friendship, and even a dog cannot bear being treated too kindly.
  \item To disregard is to win regard: attention rationed is attention valued; most people's freely distributed warmth is worth exactly what it costs.
  \item The lever runs on visible desire --- whoever can read what you want can price it out of reach or sell it back at passion's premium.
\end{distilled}
