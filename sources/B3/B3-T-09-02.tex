\dossier{B3}{T-09-02}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 1, pp. 3--20; Law 34, pp. 306--314}

\begin{distilled}
  \item Law 1 names outshining the master the worst mistake in its domain: superiors want to feel secure and superior in wit and charm, and displayed talent next to them reads as a claim on their seat.
  \item The minister case: Europe's most spectacular tribute-party was read as a rival court; the host was arrested within a day and spent twenty years in prison, and the king replaced the sparkling minister with a bland one.
  \item Masters feign appreciation; the replacement is chosen for comfort, not merit, and an excuse is always found.
  \item Inadvertent outshining: some masters are insecure enough that unguarded excellence alone offends --- no boasting is required.
  \item Law 34's crown strategy: belief in your own destiny radiates outward and infects observers, who conclude there must be grounds for it; crown-bearers show no inner sense of limits, and limits disappear from their treatment.
  \item The shrinking account: children demand everything, rebuffs build boundaries that harden, and adults end up bowing, scraping and apologising for the simplest requests --- accepting limitations that are self-imposed.
  \item Law 34's boundary: radiate confidence, not arrogance or disdain; never elevate yourself by humiliating people; do not loom too high above the crowd; and a regal pose in the wrong season is lethal --- the king who answered a reforming age with stiff kingliness lost his throne and his head.
\end{distilled}

\begin{unique}
  \item The two-sided pricing rule: the same visible excellence prices you up with peers and down with insecure superiors, so reputation management has a direction --- the entry's ceiling is the master's ego.
  \item The inadvertent case: the master's insecurity, not the agent's display, sets the threshold of offence; diagnosis must run on how the last excellent person was treated.
  \item Confidence as inferred evidence: observers supply reasons for the bearing because first-hand checking is expensive --- the mechanism the whole entry runs on.
\end{unique}
