\dossier{B3}{T-17-05}{\bookshort{B3}}{Law 37 (delivery keys), pp. 309--316}

\begin{distilled}
  \item The delivery doctrine inside the spectacle law: arrange the audience's state --- venue, sequence, symbols, hour --- before arranging the words.
  \item Dazzled by appearances, no one notices what is really being done: the state the words arrive in is part of the argument.
  \item Sequence decides memory: the first image frames the reading, the last image is what is retold.
  \item Reversal: none; but the source's general theatrical warning applies --- staging that shows is worse than none.
\end{distilled}
