\case{C-01}{The Patient Investor}{Six slow moves that bought a bakery --- and what only the sequence reveals}

\begin{story}
Priya runs two bakeries and wants a third. At a neighbourhood function she meets Mr. Okafor, a retired financier who praises her sourdough with the specificity of a man who has eaten it. He never talks money. Weeks later he makes his first move: an introduction to a flour supplier who, it happens, solves her delivery problem. He waves off thanks --- \emph{retirement hobbies should be useful}. The calls continue at his pace, never hers: a Christmas card, a name dropped, a deal he says he declined because it smelled wrong. He is never hungry. When the bank stalls her expansion in the third year, the timing is his: the loan he offers is modest, the terms \emph{friends first}, the paperwork light. She signs without counsel --- counsel would have read the clause that converts his loan to equity on any technical default, and the assignment she signs over the supplier relationship. Two years later the third bakery is thriving when a late VAT filing trips the technicality. He takes the equity with apology and precision. Her lawyer, reading the file afterwards, asks the only question that matters: why had no one priced the friendship?
\end{story}

\begin{lattice}
  \casemove{Follow the benefit, not the words}{T-05-02} The interest was traceable from the first month: the gifted introduction went to a supplier he part-owned. The money's direction was public before a single term was.
  \casemove{Patience as position}{T-02-03} He never once moved at her tempo. Three years of unhurried contact priced his need at zero and hers, by the end, at everything.
  \casemove{The unpriced account}{T-04-02} Each unasked favour opened an entry in a ledger she never audited. By bank day the balance was large enough that \emph{friends first} read as modesty rather than as leverage.
  \casemove{The dependence build}{T-11-01} His help kept arriving just ahead of each collapse --- never during stability. The schedule alone should have been the tell: rescue that arrives on schedule is supply.
  \casemove{Meekness as transport}{T-13-02} The retiree persona --- self-deprecating, bored, harmless --- collected access and lowered her guard. The question was never whether he seemed meek but what the meekness was collecting.
  \casemove{The record over the warmth}{T-06-04} Declarations said friend; the deed record said counterparty --- supplier stake, conversion clause, silence whenever she asked a terms question. The record was legible two years before the clause fired.
\end{lattice}

\begin{lesson}
No single beat was damning --- several were generous. The lattice is the finding: benefit traced one way, tempo controlled by one hand, debt accumulated off the books, dependence deepened on schedule, and the whole package wearing a meek mask. Any one audit breaks the machine; she never ran one, because warmth is an argument against auditing. The defence was one sentence at the bank day: \emph{let my lawyer price your friendship}. An operator who cannot survive that sentence has told you his terms.
\end{lesson}
