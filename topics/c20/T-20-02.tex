\topic{T-20-02}{The Dirty Way}{The indirect route after the front door closes --- and what it costs}

\begin{core}
When direct persuasion fails, the source admits a last route: go around the person --- through their spouse, their friends, their subordinates, their loyalties --- and let someone closer to them move them. It is presented, correctly, as the dirty way: it works, it is how con artists operate, and everything about it is corrosive. This entry documents it for recognition.
\end{core}
\begin{mechanism}
The source's example is a furniture salesman with a drunkard's reputation who could no longer sell honestly in his own name, so he engineered the sale through the customer's household --- making the wife the seller, so the purchase arrived as the household's own idea. The mechanism is borrowed trust: a refusal addressed to you costs the target nothing, but a refusal to a loved one costs intimacy, so operators route their request through the person whose ask cannot be refused cheaply. Secondary pressure comes from coalition display --- the target discovers that everyone around them has already agreed. The method is old because the lever is real: every person is embedded in relationships they will not spend. What the source does not soften is the classification --- this is manipulation against the will, using hostages of affection, and the book's own final chapter flags the third course of using people ruthlessly as the road it declines to write for.
\end{mechanism}
\begin{conditions}
Appears wherever the direct door is closed: cold relationships, gatekeepers, burned bridges. It is most effective against the embedded --- people with much loved ones can lose --- and weakest against the isolated, who have nobody to route through. It escalates: each use raises the operator's tolerance for the next.
\end{conditions}
\begin{application}
  \item Recognition first. The signatures: your refusals are answered by other people's sudden convictions; the request keeps arriving from different mouths; the people around you start defending the deal you declined.
  \item Map the pressure: who benefits from each messenger's ask? The beneficiary, not the messenger, is the operator.
  \item Answer the message, not the messenger --- and tell the messenger plainly what is being done to them.
  \item Break the relay: inform the whole circle at once, so no single relationship can be leveraged privately.
  \item If you are the target's friend and being used: refuse the use, and say so to the target directly.
\end{application}
\begin{feedback}
  \item Working (as defence): the relay goes quiet after exposure --- borrowed trust only works while it is invisible.
  \item Working: the operator reappears at the front door, negotiating directly. That is a win.
  \item Failing: the circle turns on you for naming it. Isolation was the design; the exposure triggered it early.
  \item Failing: you answered the messenger with anger --- the operator's script expected it and the breach is now yours.
\end{feedback}
\begin{failure}
The method's price is paid in the operator's ledger. It consumes the hostage relationships it leverages --- the spouse learns she was used as a tool, and that lesson does not stay filed. It makes the operator unemployable in trust: everyone who watches the trick wonders whose ask they are delivering next. And it is the on-ramp to the outright criminal --- the source records that this road has no natural stopping point, and much of the conduct described is criminal in most jurisdictions. The entry is admitted for recognition and defence only.
\end{failure}
\begin{countermeasures}
  \item Audit your soft touches: which of your loved ones can move you against your own judgement? That list is the operator's target map.
  \item Notice consensus that arrives too coherently --- everyone in your circle recommending the same thing with the same words is seeded, not social (T-13-01).
  \item Refuse to relay: the whole system dies if the friends it borrows decline the role.
  \item Keep one relationship with each important person that the operator cannot price --- history, not utility (T-11-01).
\end{countermeasures}
