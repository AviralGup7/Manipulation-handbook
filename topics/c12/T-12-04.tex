\topic{T-12-04}{Credit Routing}{Use the wisdom and legwork of others; take the standing, give the thanks}

\begin{core}
Never do yourself what others can do for you. The leverage move is to route other people's wisdom, knowledge and legwork into your cause --- the speed and efficiency read as godlike, the helpers are forgotten, and you are remembered. The craft's honest core is the insight that credit is a routing decision: whoever the attribution attaches to compounds; whoever it passes over compounds nothing.
\end{core}
\begin{mechanism}
B3's law and its cautionary case run together. The judgement: assistance saves time and energy, and --- the unlovely half --- the audience credits the person at the front of the work, not the hands inside it; visibility is where credit lands. Edison is the transgressionary patron and the beneficiary both: Tesla's genius ran through Edison's operation and Edison's name absorbed it --- and the same law, run backward, is Edison's own habit of taking credit for the work of those below while graciously ceding credit upward to those above. The mechanism is that credit compounds like interest: attributed achievements buy the next platform, which buys the next achievement, and the compounding attaches to whoever holds the attribution. Upward ceding is the same mechanism pointed where it pays --- the sovereign's goodwill is a platform; peers' goodwill is ambient. The law's cost is as real as its yield, and this entry keeps the ledger open on purpose: routed credit is, in the plain sense, taken credit, and every routed contributor is a future enemy with a story.
\end{mechanism}
\begin{conditions}
Strongest in attribution-opaque systems --- large organisations, layered authorship, collective output --- where contribution is genuinely hard to trace and the front man is a structural fact. Weakens where provenance is auditable (papers, code, ledgers) and collapses where the routed workers hold exit power. Ethically, the entry documents the mechanism to make it visible --- visible enough to defend against, and to refuse.
\end{conditions}
\begin{application}
  \item Notice where credit lands by default in your system, and who is structurally positioned at the landing site.
  \item If you lead, route credit downward deliberately and visibly --- it buys more than it costs, and the compounding runs on delivery, not talk.
  \item If you are being routed, put attribution into the artefact: named authorship, recorded contribution, provenance that travels with the work.
  \item When a front man absorbs collective work, decide fast whether the trade --- their platform for your substance --- is worth renegotiating.
  \item Never dispute credit in the moment it is taken; document it, and present it at the moment of renewal --- promotion, funding, partnership.
\end{application}
\begin{feedback}
  \item Working: your name now precedes your work in rooms you have not entered. The compounding has started.
  \item Working (as leader): your best people stop job-hunting. Routed-down credit is retention.
  \item Failing: you are doing everything yourself and tired. The law's first sentence is addressed to you.
  \item Failing: contributors have stopped giving you their best work. The routing is being charged in kind.
\end{feedback}
\begin{failure}
The taken-credit account is real and compounds too: every routed contributor holds a story in which you stole, and the story is told at your renewal moments. Edison's name survived Tesla, but the organisation that understands what happened prices its Edisons carefully afterwards. And the front-of-house habit, run too long, hollows the substance it advertises --- a person known for everything eventually has to appear somewhere without their crew.
\end{failure}
\begin{countermeasures}
  \item Audit attribution in your own shop annually: whose names attach to what? The routing you did not choose is being done to you.
  \item When someone's achievements never name collaborators, read the silence as data --- and check one contributor's version before you hire the headline.
  \item Record provenance as you work: drafts, commits, minutes. The retrospective routing cannot survive a timestamp.
  \item If you must share credit upward, do it in exchange for platform, explicitly. The gracious cession is a sale; make sure something is bought.
\end{countermeasures}
