\topic{T-23-02}{Manufactured Rapport}{Detecting liking that has been assembled}


\begin{core}
Rapport that has been built to order shows a characteristic shape: it arrives fast, it is one-directional, it matches you specifically, and it is withdrawn when it stops being useful. Genuine rapport is slower, less flattering and survives disagreement.
\end{core}

\begin{mechanism}
The detection works because manufactured liking is optimised rather than developed. It is produced to a specification --- your vocabulary, your pace, your opinions, your background --- and anything optimised to a target overshoots it. Real resemblance is partial and includes friction; matched resemblance is total and includes none.

Speed is the most reliable single signal. Familiarity takes time to accumulate, and warmth that arrives without the time has been supplied rather than earned. Direction is the second: manufactured rapport flows toward you and asks little in return, because its function is to lower your defences rather than to build a relationship. The third test is disagreement --- introduce one, mildly. Rapport that survives a difference of opinion is probably real. Rapport that immediately re-matches you is not.
\end{mechanism}

\begin{application}
  \item Introduce a mild disagreement and watch whether it is engaged or absorbed.
  \item Change one of your own positions mid-conversation and see whether theirs follows.
  \item Ask something personal and unrelated to the business; a manufactured relationship has no off-script material.
  \item Delay the decision by a day and observe whether the warmth persists without progress.
  \item Compare their manner toward you with their manner toward someone who cannot help them.
\end{application}

\begin{feedback}
  \item Warmth arrives in the first minutes rather than over weeks.
  \item The resemblance is comprehensive and includes your vocabulary, pace and opinions.
  \item They agree with you on matters where disagreement would cost them nothing.
  \item Praise is specific, early, and precedes any request.
  \item The warmth cools immediately after the decision is made.
  \item Nothing about them surprises you, which is unusual in a person you have just met.
\end{feedback}

\begin{failure}
Run as a general posture this diagnostic is expensive: it makes ordinary friendliness suspicious and produces a person who cannot accept warmth without auditing it, which costs real relationships and eventually makes the suspicion self-confirming. It is worth running where there is a transaction pending, where the warmth arrived unusually fast, or where you have something the other party wants. Elsewhere let it pass.
\end{failure}

\begin{countermeasures}
  \item When they claim shared background, ask a specific question about it. Invented common ground does not survive detail.
  \item When praise arrives early, receive it neutrally and change the subject; do not reciprocate, since reciprocity is what is being purchased.
  \item When the warmth cools after the decision, note the timing rather than raising it --- the pattern is the information and it will repeat.
  \item When a mild disagreement is instantly absorbed, agree and re-open the substantive question separately.
\end{countermeasures}
