\topic{T-18-04}{Behave Like Others}{Conformity as camouflage}

\begin{core}
Think what you like; behave like the people around you. Visible difference draws audit, and a new or weak position cannot pay the audit's price. The move is camouflage: conform outwardly --- manners, rituals, vocabulary, unremarkable opinions --- while the real project grows quietly, because the different thing is attacked in proportion to its visibility and only becomes safe when it is too established to unmake.
\end{core}
\begin{mechanism}
Groups read visible deviation as a claim of rank, and they discipline it long before they evaluate it --- the man who declares his heresy in his first year is destroyed, while the same doctrine, arriving wrapped in orthodoxy, gets a hearing. The mechanism is attention economics: every group has limited tolerance for novelty per season, and outlier behaviour spends it on the surface, where nothing can be built. The canonical case is the philosopher imprisoned for his beliefs who stopped writing declarations and started advising the power that held him --- the doctrine survived by entering through service, not proclamation. Conformity is thus not agreement but armour: it buys safety for the interior difference while the interior difference compounds. The doctrine is preached only after the win is secured, when the audience can no longer unmake the preacher.
\end{mechanism}
\begin{conditions}
Strongest for newcomers, minorities-of-one, and anyone holding a belief the room punishes --- also strongest in strong-culture institutions with dense rituals. Weakens in founding moments, where difference is the product, and in loose, low-surveillance settings where nobody is keeping the list. Fatal misuse: conformity that quietly becomes the self --- the camouflage that never comes off.
\end{conditions}
\begin{application}
  \item On arrival, match the surface completely: dress, ritual, pace, the room's acceptable opinions.
  \item Keep your difference functional --- visible in work, invisible in manners --- and let results, not declarations, carry it.
  \item Acquire one local ally before any visible deviation; a difference introduced by a member reads differently.
  \item Drop the camouflage incrementally, and only where you have accumulated enough standing to absorb the audit.
  \item Never announce the mask. The moment your conformity is described as strategy, it stops working as one.
\end{application}
\begin{feedback}
  \item Working: you are trusted with the room's ordinary business while your real project advances unexamined.
  \item Working: your difference, when it surfaces, is met with curiosity instead of discipline.
  \item Failing: you are invited to every ritual and no decision. Camouflage succeeded; inclusion did not follow.
  \item Failing: the mask is named --- \emph{you're only telling us what we want to hear}.
\end{feedback}
\begin{failure}
The price is paid by the self: long camouflage trains the performer, and the difference it protected can atrophy from disuse. It also reads as cowardice to allies who carried the visible flag while you blended --- alliances made under a mask are repayable in trust. And the strategy is hostage to the host: camouflage without an exit plan ends when the host turns, and turning is a season, not a surprise.
\end{failure}
\begin{countermeasures}
  \item In your own group, notice who agrees with everything and produces quietly. Assess their real position before you assess their loyalty.
  \item Distinguish camouflage from sycophancy: the first protects a difference you can name; the second has nothing behind it. Ask what the agreement is protecting.
  \item Track your own drift: keep the heresy written down somewhere outside the host's reach (T-12-05).
  \item When someone finally drops the mask, grade the doctrine, not the timing --- and ask what the timing was protecting them from.
\end{countermeasures}
